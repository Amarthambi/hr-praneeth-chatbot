# Annual Leave

## Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave each calendar year.
Part-time employees receive a pro-rated entitlement based on their contracted hours.
Annual leave is available after the employee completes one month of service.

## How to apply

Submit an annual leave request in the HR portal at least 5 working days before the start date.
The line manager approves or declines the request in the portal.
Leave is not confirmed until the portal shows an approved status.

## Carry-over

Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried into the next calendar year.
Carried days must be used by 31 March.
Days not used by 31 March expire and are not paid out.
