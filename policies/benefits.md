# Employee Benefits

## Housing benefit

The housing benefit is a monthly allowance for eligible employees.
Full-time employees who have completed 6 months of service can qualify.
The employee's primary workplace must be more than 40 kilometres from their home.
Eligible employees receive a housing allowance of 8000 each month.
Part-time employees are not eligible for the housing benefit.
Employees who are still in their first 6 months of service are not eligible for the housing benefit.
HR confirms eligibility from the home address and workplace recorded in the HR portal.

## Health cover

All employees are enrolled in the company health plan from their start date.
Dependants can be added by submitting the dependant form in the HR portal.

## Transport allowance

Employees who commute more than 20 kilometres each way may claim a transport allowance.
The transport allowance is 2000 each month.
Submit the claim in the HR portal with the home and workplace addresses.
