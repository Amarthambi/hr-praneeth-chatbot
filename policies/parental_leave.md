# Parental Leave

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed 12 months of continuous service may apply for parental leave.
The policy applies to birth, adoption, and long-term foster placement.

## Application process

Tell your manager and HR at least 8 weeks before the leave start date.
Submit the parental leave form in the HR portal with the expected start date and end date.
Attach the birth, adoption, or placement document when it is available.
HR confirms eligibility in writing within 10 working days.
Agree a return-to-work date with your manager before the leave begins.

## Duration

A primary caregiver may take 16 weeks of parental leave.
A secondary caregiver may take 4 weeks of parental leave.
The first 8 weeks for a primary caregiver are paid at the normal salary.
The remaining parental leave is unpaid unless a separate company scheme says otherwise.
