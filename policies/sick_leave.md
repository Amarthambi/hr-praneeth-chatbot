# Sick Leave

## Entitlement

Employees may take up to 10 days of paid sick leave in each calendar year.
Unused sick leave does not carry into the next year.

## Medical documentation

Medical documentation is required when sick leave lasts more than 2 consecutive working days.
The document must be a certificate from a registered medical practitioner.
Upload the certificate in the HR portal on the third working day of the absence.
Medical documentation is not required for 1 working day of sick leave.
Medical documentation is not required for 2 consecutive working days of sick leave.

## How to report

Notify your manager before the shift starts on the first day of absence.
Record the absence in the HR portal on the same day.
